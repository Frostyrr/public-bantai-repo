## Why Bantai?

Managing organization finances involves more than keeping a spreadsheet. Members need to know what they owe, officers need supporting records for every expense, and teams need a clear history of who submitted, verified, and approved each decision.

Bantai brings those workflows into one transparent workspace: collections, dues, budgets, expenses, reimbursements, cash advances, liquidation, and financial reporting. Personal dashboards help members track their own contributions, while organization workspaces give officers the tools to manage shared records with built-in accountability.

## What you can do

| Area                               | Capabilities                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Organizations & membership**     | Create or join organizations using invite codes, manage member roles, and switch between workspaces.                                      |
| **Collections & member directory** | Create collection campaigns, manage the member directory, assign dues, record payments, view receipts, and track outstanding obligations. |
| **Budgets & expenses**             | Allocate budgets, monitor utilization, submit expenses with supporting receipts, and follow independent verification and approval.        |
| **Reimbursements**                 | Submit reimbursement claims with receipt attachments, review requests, and track payouts.                                                 |
| **Cash advances & liquidation**    | Track released advances, submitted liquidations, review decisions, and unresolved accountability.                                         |
| **Reports & audit history**        | View financial summaries and audit activity, organized by reporting periods and spaces.                                                   |
| **Personal workspace**             | Follow personal contributions and dues across organizations, and manage account preferences.                                              |
| **Bani AI assistant**              | Ask questions about organization records, receive conversational financial summaries, and analyze uploaded receipt images.                |

> **Note:** Bantai records and organizes financial activity; actual payments and money transfers happen outside the platform. Reported balances reflect the records entered into Bantai and should not be treated as a reconciled bank statement.

## From contribution to accountability

1. **Create an account** with email verification or Google sign-in.
2. **Create or join a workspace**, then invite members and assign roles.
3. **Organize financial activity** through collections, budgets, expenses, reimbursements, and cash advances.
4. **Review independently.** Standard policy requires an independent officer to verify and approve. Owners can configure an explicit Owner/Admin self-approval exception with justification, or Strict mode requiring two distinct independent approvers. Each expense retains its policy at submission.
5. **Keep the team informed** through personal contribution views, financial summaries, and audit history.

Available actions depend on organization membership and role (`owner`, `admin`, `treasurer`, `auditor`, `member`, or `student`), enforced by the backend and database policies.

## Meet Bani

**Bani helps your team make sense of its financial records.** Inside an organization workspace, ask questions such as:

> “How are our collections progressing?”
>
> “Which expenses are awaiting review?”
>
> “How much of our budget have we used?”

Bani retrieves live, organization-scoped data covering collections, dues, budgets, expenses, treasury summaries, and cash advances, and can also extract details from uploaded receipt images to speed up expense and reimbursement entry. Conversations are saved so users can return to them anytime.

Bani is a **read-only assistant**: it cannot approve expenses, record payments, or change budgets. Officers remain responsible for all financial decisions and updates. The public homepage also includes an interactive preview using sample data.
